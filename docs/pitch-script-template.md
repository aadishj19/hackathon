# Pitch script

Works for a live pitch or a recorded video. Check the time limit with the organisers and plan to finish a little under it. Rough length guide: 150 spoken words per minute.

## Story beats

**1. The person (about 20 seconds)**
Who they are and the moment it goes wrong for them. One real-sounding example beats a statistic.
> [text]

On screen: [what the audience sees]

**2. What we built (one sentence)**
> [text]

On screen: [the app's first screen]

**3. Demo (most of the time)**
Follow the demo script step by step. For each step, say what the user gets out of it, not which button you press.
> [text]

On screen: [the app]

**4. Why KBC should believe it (about 30 seconds)**
The accuracy number and how it was measured, the time or money it saves, and how personal data stays protected with a person making the final call.
> [text]

On screen: [one slide or the app's numbers]

**5. What's next (one or two sentences)**
The first step towards a pilot at KBC.
> [text]

## Before going on stage or pressing record
- Rehearse with a timer at least twice.
- Only use demo inputs you have already tried.
- Click "Reload data" in the app for a clean start, and keep the backup recording ready.
